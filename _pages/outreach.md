I am interested in the social side of astronomy. I strongly believe that everyone should have access to astronomy and that everyone should feel respected and safe within the astronomy community. Below are outreach and DEI activities in which I have been involved over the years.

## [RECA mentorship program](https://www.astroreca.org/en/mentoring)

From 2020 to 2023, I have led a mentorship program for undergraduate physics and astronomy students in Colombia. The program aims to guide students as they pursue their professional goals. These include an academic career in astronomy, a career in education, or a path in industry. The program is now stablish as part of the RECA student network.

## [RECA internship program](https://www.astroreca.org/en/internship)

In the summer of 2021, I co-organized the astronomy internship program with the Colombian Network of Students (RECA). During the 10-week program, 13 students in Colombia carried out research projects with astronomers from around the world. This online program provided an opportunity for students to work directly with astronomers worldwide.

[Press release from one of the main newspapers in Colombia](https://www.elespectador.com/ciencia/debut-en-la-gran-cancha-del-universo/)

## Diversity Journal Club at Steward Observatory

I used to organize the [Diversity Journal Club](https://www.as.arizona.edu/diversity_coffee) in the Department of Astronomy at Steward Observatory, together with [Samantha Scibelli](https://samscibelli.github.io/), Erin Maier, and [Carlos Vargas](https://www.as.arizona.edu/people/postdoctoral/carlos-j-vargas). This was a space created to engage with the community at all levels on topics of equity, inclusion, and diversity.

## Astrobitos

<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/outreach/astrobitos.png" width="600">

I have been a writer for Astrobitos since 2017. Astrobitos is the Spanish version of Astrobites. We write summaries of astronomy papers three times per week. Astrobitos members are astronomy graduate students and postdocs.

## Astronomia periferica

<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/outreach/astronomiaperiferica.jpg" width="600">

Astronomia periferica was a program funded by the IAU [Office of Astronomy for Development](http://www.astro4dev.org/) that took place in Bogota, Colombia. Astronomia periferica was aimed at children from underprivileged neighborhoods in Bogotá. We carried out several hands-on activities involving science and art, motivated by physical concepts such as light, dimensions, and space. Our final project involved building two exoplanets with different "atmospheres"; this project was presented by the children and became a one-month exhibition at the Planetarium of Bogota.
