I am interested in the social side of astronomy. I strongly believe that everyone
should have access to astronomy and everyone should feel respected and safe inside the
astronomy community. Below are outreach and DEI activities
in which I have been involved over the years. 

## [Vision Cosmica Podcast (in Spanish)](https://cosmoquest.org/x/visioncosmica/)

From time to time, I also collaborate on the podcast Vision Cosmica, a podcast about astronomy in Spanish produced by [cosmoquest](https://cosmoquest.org). 



## [RECA mentorship program](https://www.astroreca.org/en/mentoring)

Since August 2020, I've been leading a mentorship program for undergraduate physics and astronomy students in Colombia. The program aims to guide students as they pursue their professional goals. These include an academic career in astronomy, a career in education, or a path in industry. So far, the program has had over 60 students! 

## [RECA internship program](https://www.astroreca.org/en/internship) 

In the summer of 2021, I co-organized the astronomy internship program with the Colombian Network of Students (RECA). During the 10-week program, 13 students in Colombia carried out research projects with astronomers from around the world. This online program provided an opportunity for students to work directly with astronomers worldwide. 

[Press release from one of the main newspapers in Colombia](https://www.elespectador.com/ciencia/debut-en-la-gran-cancha-del-universo/)

## Diversity Journal Club at Steward Observatory:

I used to organize, along with [Samantha Scibelli](https://samscibelli.github.io/), Erin Maier, and
[Carlos Vargas](https://www.as.arizona.edu/people/postdoctoral/carlos-j-vargas)
the [Diversity Journal Club](https://www.as.arizona.edu/diversity_coffee)
in the Department of Astronomy at Steward Observatory. This was a space created
to engage with the community at all levels on topics of equity, inclusion, and
diversity.

## Astrobitos


<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/outreach/astrobitos.png" width="600">

I've been a writer for Astrobitos since 2017. 
Astrobitos is the Spanish version of Astrobites. We write summaries of
astronomy papers three times per week. Astrobitos members are all
astronomy graduate students and postdocs. 

## Astronomia periferica


| <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/outreach/astronomiaperiferica.jpg" width="400"> | Astronomia periferica was a program funded by the IAU [Office of Astronomy for Development](http://www.astro4dev.org/) that took place in Bogota, Colombia. Astronomia periferica was targeted at kids from underprivileged neighborhoods in Bogotá. We did several hands-on activities that involved science and art, motivated by physical concepts such as light, dimensions, and space. Our final project involved building two exoplanets with different "atmospheres"; this project was presented by the kids and became a one-month exhibition in the Planetarium of Bogota. |
