### nba: N-body Analysis

[nba](https://github.com/jngaravitoc/nba) is a Python package to analyze N-body simulations of galaxies:
reading snapshots, finding the centers of halos and satellites, density and kinematic profiles, orbits, and
sky maps. It was written for the MW-LMC simulations of
[Garavito-Camargo et al. (2021)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2021ApJ...919..109G) and works with any
Gadget-4 HDF5 snapshot.

- **Documentation:** [jngaravitoc.github.io/nba](https://jngaravitoc.github.io/nba/), with tutorials on
  reading the GC21 simulations and following the LMC along its orbit.
- **Code:** [github.com/jngaravitoc/nba](https://github.com/jngaravitoc/nba), MIT license.
- **Install:** `git clone https://github.com/jngaravitoc/nba.git && pip install ./nba`
